      pushpc

      What is pushpc? 🤔

      The pushpc command forces the currently attached player character to move (knock-back) backwards or inside a specific direction by a designated number of map cells.

      This knock-back is ignoring standard character protection parameters like items or map flags (i.e. it is unstoppable like Boss knock-backs). However, movement is physically blocked by structural layout obstacles such as building structures or mountain walls. If the player is pushed into an obstacle, they move only up to that boundary wall and stop safely.

      Syntax of pushpc 📜

      • pushpc <direction>,<cells>;

      Parameters 🎯

      • <direction>: The directional constant indicating where the player will be pushed. Constants are declared in src/map/script_constants.hpp:
        • - DIR_NORTH (0)
        • - DIR_NORTHEAST (1)
        • - DIR_EAST (2)
        • - DIR_SOUTHEAST (3)
        • - DIR_SOUTH (4)
        • - DIR_SOUTHWEST (5)
        • - DIR_WEST (6)
        • - DIR_NORTHWEST (7)
      • <cells>: The total distance of squares to knock back the character.

      Example Usage 💡

      Example Code:
      //===== rAthena Script =======================================
//= Gust Trap NPC Simulation
//============================================================
prontera,150,150,4	script	Wind Trap	139,{
    // Check if player steps on the trap coordinate area
    mes "^FF0000[Trap Triggered]^000000";
    mes "Whoosh! You are hit by a sudden, intense squall of gusting winds!";
    close2; // Disconnect overlay to let visual action play out below
    
    // Knocks back the character 5 cells in the East direction
    pushpc DIR_EAST, 5;
    end;
}

      In this script:

      • On line 10, when the player interacts or hits the wind trap script, pushpc DIR_EAST, 5 instantly shifts their position 5 tiles to the East, simulating a mechanical trap strike.
